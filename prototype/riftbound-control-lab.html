Riftbound Control Lab

  • Drag below the rune line to move (relative). Touch or drag in the arena to set the aim angle; it stays locked when you lift your finger.
  • Lyra starts with the Ember Ball (her element ball) and 3 white baby balls with no element.
  • 4 ball slots and 4 item slots. Once full, level-ups only offer upgrades for what you own. Ball upgrades are random: an extra copy or a stat increase.
  • Balls absorb green gems, which become XP when the ball returns. Rainbow Rift Prisms (from brutes and bosses) fuse two Lv3 balls. One slot becomes empty.
  • Flame button bottom right: ultimate. II button: pause, slots and settings.

On desktop: WASD/arrows to move, Q/E to rotate aim, Space for ultimate, Esc to pause.
